About DialogBuddy

We believe you learn to speak English by speaking it — with real people, in real conversations, not through endless drills.

Our mission

Most English learners can read and write far better than they can speak. The gap isn't knowledge — it's practice and confidence. DialogBuddy exists to close that gap by making live speaking practice easy, friendly, and low-pressure.

How it works

Create an account, tell us your English level and interests, and we match you with a partner for a live voice call. No scripts, no grades — just conversation. You can also add friends, chat, and call people you've enjoyed talking to.

Who it's for

DialogBuddy is for anyone who wants to speak English more fluently and confidently — students, working professionals, job seekers, and lifelong learners. Whether you're a beginner or polishing near-native fluency, you'll find partners at your level.

Why speaking practice matters

Speaking is a skill, and skills improve with use. Regular real conversations build vocabulary recall, pronunciation, listening comprehension, and — most importantly — the confidence to speak without hesitation.
