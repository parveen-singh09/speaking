Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about practicing English speaking with DialogBuddy.

How can I practice English speaking?

The most effective way to practice English speaking is to have real conversations as often as possible. DialogBuddy matches you with a partner at your level for live voice calls, so you can practice speaking naturally without scripts or pressure. Daily short conversations build fluency faster than studying grammar alone.

How do I learn to speak English fluently?

Fluency comes from regular, active use of the language. Speak rather than only read or listen, practice with real people, learn vocabulary in context, and don't fear mistakes. Fifteen minutes of real conversation every day improves fluency steadily.

How can I improve my English speaking at home for free?

You can improve your spoken English at home for free by practicing with conversation partners online, thinking in English, reading aloud, and recording yourself to check pronunciation. DialogBuddy is free to start and connects you with real partners for live voice practice from anywhere.

How do I overcome the fear of speaking English?

Fear fades with practice in a safe, judgment-free setting. Start with friendly partners who are also learning, keep conversations low-pressure, and remember mistakes are a normal part of learning.

How can I improve my English pronunciation?

Improve pronunciation by listening closely to native and fluent speakers, repeating what you hear, and getting feedback during real conversations. Speaking with partners on DialogBuddy lets you hear natural pronunciation and practice your own in context, which is far more effective than silent study.

What is DialogBuddy and how does it work?

DialogBuddy is a platform for practicing spoken English with real people. You create an account, set your English level and interests, and get matched with a partner for a live voice call. You can also add friends, chat, and call them directly to keep practicing.

Is DialogBuddy free to use?

Yes. You can create an account and start having English conversations on DialogBuddy for free, using your email or Google account.

How long does it take to become fluent in English?

The time to fluency depends on your starting level, how often you practice, and how much you speak. Learners who have regular spoken conversations typically progress much faster than those who only study. Daily speaking practice is the single biggest accelerator of fluency.
